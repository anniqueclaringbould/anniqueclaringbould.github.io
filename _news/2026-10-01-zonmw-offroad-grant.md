Very happy to share that my grant proposal ***From polygenic scores to regulatory profiles: Evaluating sequence-based AI in human β-cells*** has been funded by ZonMw's [Off Road grant](https://www.zonmw.nl/en/news/road-grant-24-innovative-ideas)!

🚀 With the recent development of AI sequence-to-function models, the central question is: can we combine variant-level predictions into a patient-specific regulatory profile for type 2 diabetes risk, and then test it in the lab in human β-cells? 

🎯 Testing these predictions will show what these models get right and where they fall short, shaping experimental design and research questions in the future.

[Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/)'s new AlphaGenome Atlas couldn't have come out at a better time. Exciting times ahead!
